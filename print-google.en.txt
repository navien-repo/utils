  |_| |_/_/   \_\_____|    Google Admin

  ==========================================================

   You are about to create the service account HAL uses to
   read your Google Workspace. Read-only. Nothing leaves this
   terminal: at the end you take one file to HAL yourself.

   How it works

     1) Confirm creating the account         <-- you are here
     2) Download the file hal-google-admin.json
     3) Upload the file in HAL
     4) Authorize the account in the Admin console

  ----------------------------------------------------------

   STEP 1

   Type the command below and press Enter to confirm creating
   the HAL-Project project and the hal-admin-sa service
   account:

       ./create_hal_service_account en
